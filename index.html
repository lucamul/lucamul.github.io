About

I'm a backend engineer at SICPA, where I've spent the last three years designing distributed, event-driven systems for payments, digital identity, and product traceability. Alongside that I do applied research into cryptography and privacy-preserving technology, prototyping ideas before they become products.

I hold an MSc in Cyber Security from ETH Zürich, jointly with EPFL, where I focused on applied cryptography, distributed systems, and formal verification. My master's thesis on distributed transaction protocols was awarded the ETH Medal, and the work continued into three papers written with my former advisors, on protocol design and formal verification of database isolation guarantees.

Day to day I write Java, Kotlin, Rust, and Python most often, though I've worked in C, C++, and Scala too. Outside of work I build small systems software for fun, most recently a terminal text editor in Rust, and I keep experimenting with data science and machine learning applied to security problems.
